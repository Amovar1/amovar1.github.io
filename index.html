Export format: used by every download
Whatever you set here is used by every ⬇ button on the samples, by the download button in the editor, and by “All as ZIP”.

Sliders apply when you press Generate / re-roll (or turn on Auto re-roll). Samples you have edited are kept; “Re-roll all” discards the edits. Ghost tone, Normalize and the Export settings apply instantly.

Individual hit volume: one bar per hit. Click or drag across the bars (up = louder).
Overall envelope: runs from the start until the sound drops 50 dB below its peak. Click to add a point, drag to move, right-click a point to delete it.
